PolyInstall bundles or depends on the following third-party components (see individual licenses in NuGet packages):

- YamlDotNet (YAML parsing in the CLI)
- Avalonia, Avalonia.Themes.Fluent, Avalonia.Desktop (installer UI)
- Microsoft.Extensions.FileSystemGlobbing (manifest glob resolution)
- Json.Schema (JsonSchema.Net) and associated packages (CLI manifest validation)
- NJsonSchema (schema generation tool in PolyInstall.SchemaGen)
- AppImage type-2 runtime ELF from AppImageKit (downloaded at build time when producing AppImages; see https://github.com/AppImage/AppImageKit )
